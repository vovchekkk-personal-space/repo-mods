**Description:**
Music Box sound replacer with "You Spin Me Round" Chorus loop by Dead or Alive

Credit to [RENNY](https://thunderstore.io/c/repo/p/RENNY/) for the idea with their [You_Spin_Me_Round_Music_Box](https://thunderstore.io/c/repo/p/RENNY/You_Spin_Me_Round_Music_Box/)
This mod is, instead, a perfect loop of the chrous of the song, rather than a longer clip.
